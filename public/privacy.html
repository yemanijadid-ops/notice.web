Privacy Policy

Short launch version. Last updated: 2026-11-18. Replace [CONTACT EMAIL] before launch.

What we collect

Account: your email address and a protected form of your password (your browser scrambles the password before it is sent, and we store only a further scrambled version, never the password itself), plus a hashed recovery code. Guests have no email or password; they get a random private identifier.
Your content: papers, atlases, journal entries, settings, and the photos and voice recordings you add.
Terms: the date and version of the terms you accepted.

We do not collect anything else about you, and we do not use advertising or tracking cookies. Your sign-in session is kept in your browser so you stay signed in.

Why

Only to run the service: to sign you in, store your content and show it back to you, and apply your plan's limits.

Where it is stored and who handles it

Your data is stored with our service providers: Cloudflare (website hosting and database). Your photos and recordings are kept in the same private database and can only be opened while you are signed in to your account. Data may be processed in other countries than yours. We do not sell your data or share it for advertising.

Your choices and rights

You can edit or delete your content at any time. You can delete your whole account from Settings, which permanently removes your data. Depending on where you live (for example in the EU or UK) you may also have rights to access, correct, export or erase your data and to object to its use: write to us and we will help.

Security

Guest accounts and their data are deleted automatically after 30 days without a visit. We keep a short-lived record of recent sign-in attempts and your connection address only to slow down guessing and spam; it is deleted within a day.

Each account can only reach its own data, connections are encrypted, and files are private. No online service is perfectly safe, so please use a strong, unique password.

Children

Notice Board is not meant for young children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.
